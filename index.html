ESCAPE

VISUAL RULE TESTS

01 — SIGIL SEED

Rule: The sigil is built from simple geometric forms that can repeat, rotate, and transform while remaining recognizable.

02 — BEHAVIOR RULE

Rule: Forms gradually dissolve when activated. The interaction begins a slow expansion and fade rather than an immediate disappearance. After testing the timing, I kept the dissolve speed because the slower transition felt controlled rather than abrupt.

03 — GESTURE LANGUAGE

Rule: The form responds to the user's proximity rather than a direct click. Moving closer increases the form's tension, while moving away allows it to settle.
